# Offer

One-line purpose: states what's actually being sold, so every asset ends
in a CTA that matches reality instead of a guessed one.

> **Skills MUST NOT run against an unfilled template.** Fill every section
> below before any skill reads this tenant.

## Products

What's actually for sale — the concrete list, not a mission statement.

## Pricing

Model and range (free/paid tiers, one-time vs. subscription, starting
price). Enough for content to reference pricing accurately.

## Promise

The outcome this brand commits to delivering if the customer buys.

## CTAs

The specific calls-to-action content should drive toward (e.g. "book a
demo," "start free trial," "join the waitlist"), by funnel stage if they
differ.
