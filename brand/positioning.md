# Positioning

One-line purpose: fixes how this brand is described relative to the
market, so content doesn't drift into generic category language.

> **Skills MUST NOT run against an unfilled template.** Fill every section
> below before any skill reads this tenant.

## Category

What this is, in the customer's own words for "what kind of thing is this."

## Differentiation

The one or two things that make this brand not-interchangeable with
alternatives.

## Proof

Evidence backing the differentiation claim: results, credentials, data,
testimonials.

## Competitors and why us

Who the customer compares this brand against, and the honest reason to
choose this one instead.

## What we're not

Explicit non-claims — positioning by exclusion, to keep content from
overreaching.
