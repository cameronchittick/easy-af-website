# ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

One-line purpose: defines who content is written for, so strategy and
drafting skills target real pains instead of generic ones.

> **Skills MUST NOT run against an unfilled template.** Fill every section
> below before any skill reads this tenant.

## Who

Role, demographics, company/context. Be specific enough to picture one
person, not a market segment.

## Pains

The problems that make them go looking for something like this brand.

## Jobs-to-be-done

What they're actually trying to accomplish — the outcome, not the feature.

## Watering holes

Where they spend attention: communities, publications, search behavior,
social platforms, events.

## Objections

Reasons they hesitate or say no. What has to be true for them to trust
this brand.

## Buying trigger

The moment or event that turns a passive prospect into an active one.
