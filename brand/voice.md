# Voice

One-line purpose: pins the tone every skill writes in, so output is
consistent across surfaces without re-briefing each time.

> **Skills MUST NOT run against an unfilled template.** Fill every section
> below before any skill reads this tenant.

## Tone words

3-5 adjectives that describe how this brand sounds. (e.g. "direct, warm,
a little irreverent")

## Cadence

Sentence and paragraph rhythm. Short and punchy, or long and explanatory?
How long are paragraphs? Contractions okay?

## Vocabulary — do

Words, phrases, and framings this brand reaches for.

## Vocabulary — don't

Words, phrases, jargon, or cliches this brand avoids.

## Sample sentences

3-5 sentences (real or written for this purpose) that sound unmistakably
like this brand.

## Point of view

First person plural ("we"), singular founder voice, or third person?
